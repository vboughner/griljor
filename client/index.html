How to Play

Movement

Use WASD or arrow keys to move. Right-click a tile to walk toward it.

Combat

Left-click on a weapon to pick it up and arm yourself. Left-click on the map to fire your weapon or use a held item.

Items

Press G to pick up an item at your feet (or left-click it). Press B to drop what you're holding. Click inventory slots to equip items.

Chat

Press T to type a message, Enter to send, Escape to cancel.

Respawn & Leave

If you get trapped, click Respawn to return to a starting room. Click Leave to exit. Both will drop all your carried items.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Movement (8 directions)

  • Q W E — NW, North, NE
  • A  •  D — West, East
  • Z S C — SW, South, SE

Arrow keys and numpad also work.

Items

  • G — Pick up item at your feet
  • B — Drop held item

Toggles

  • V — Fog of war overlay
  • P — Highlight nearby pickups
  • O — Player indicator boxes
  • I — Tile debug hover

Mouse

  • Left-click — Fire weapon / pick up / use item
  • Right-click — Walk toward tile
